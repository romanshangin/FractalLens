## Commit and artifact identifiers

History cleanup changes affected/descendant commit IDs; full maps stay private.
Reports/commits/manifests retain measured revision IDs, possibly absent here.
A dirty experiment cannot be reconstructed from base commit alone.

Never substitute sanitized commit IDs for measured revisions/checksums or claim
new objects were measured. Source/build hashes identify original inputs; artifact
hashes identify original bytes, not guaranteed redacted checksums. Removed
signatures cannot authenticate rewritten commits; originals stay private.
New docs do not change measurement identities.

## Reproduction limits and new runs

Selected evidence explains conclusions, not complete replay. Excluded samples,
frozen candidates, launch records/diagnostics prevent some recomputation here.
Dated commands may require private revisions/inputs; they are not current
copy-and-paste instructions.

New measurements use current protocol, available revision and fresh output.
Record revision/hashes/dependencies/workload/power/thermal/exact-output checks;
keep raw evidence private. Never overwrite/continue historical campaigns with
new measurements.

Review selected public evidence for secrets/prohibited machine data. Keep logs/
dumps private; publish only necessary reviewed sanitized summaries/numbers and
state replay gaps. Secret scans do not prove all private data was found.
