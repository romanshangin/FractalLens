FractalLens runtime third-party license inventory
===============================================

This directory contains the license and notice texts for every third-party
runtime component added by the FractalLens packaging pipeline. The source
revision is pinned to the binary version or embedded native revision.

Shaderc bundled by lwjgl-shaderc 3.4.2
----------------------------------------

The native JARs identify the embedded source revision in their adjacent `.git`
metadata entries. Shaderc's two third-party license files are included with its
own license.

Source commit: d5f08ae5c5a9a45165578445cbd0f9adf0223448
Files:

* Shaderc-LICENSE.txt

The linked JDK runtime retains its own legal notices under the runtime image's
`legal` directory. FractalLens's Apache License 2.0 text is staged from the
repository root as `LICENSE`. This inventory covers third-party components;
they retain their respective licenses.
